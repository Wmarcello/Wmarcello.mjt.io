Product Description

The Titan Electric Pump Series offers reliable and durable centrifugal pumping solutions for a variety of industrial applications. Since 2025, Titan Electric has been committed to delivering high-performance electric power systems, ensuring operational excellence and efficiency across industries.

  • Heavy-duty construction for long-term reliability
  • Optimized hydraulic design for maximum efficiency
  • Available in 2-pole and 4-pole configurations
  • Low maintenance, high-performance sealing options
  • Ideal for water, chemical, and HVAC systems
