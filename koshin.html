Product Description

The Koshin GL Gear Pump is a compact and robust solution for oil transfer, chemical processing, and industrial lubrication systems.

  • Positive displacement rotary gear pump
  • Cast iron body with precision-machined gears
  • Compact design suitable for fixed or portable systems
  • Excellent self-priming capability
  • High viscosity handling capability
